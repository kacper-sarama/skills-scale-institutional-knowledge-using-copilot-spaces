# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Scrum Master

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate Agile ceremonies (standups, sprint planning, retrospectives), remove team blockers, and foster continuous improvement. They enable teams to operate efficiently within the Agile framework.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, retrospectives, sprint planning, and sprint reviews
- Identify, track, and help remove impediments and blockers
- Coach the team on Agile best practices and methodologies
- Maintain sprint board and backlog health
- Support process improvement through retrospective action items

### Goals
- Ensure smooth team workflow and rhythm
- Maximize team velocity and predictability
- Foster psychological safety and continuous learning

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- One-on-ones with team members experiencing blockers
- Retrospective facilitation and action item tracking

### Interactions with Other Roles
Works closely with Developers to unblock technical impediments, coordinates with Project Managers on timeline/resource constraints, and aligns with Product Managers on backlog prioritization and sprint readiness.

---

## UX/UI Designer

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers create user experience workflows, wireframes, and visual design assets. They ensure products are usable, accessible, and aligned with user needs and brand standards.

### Responsibilities
- Create wireframes, user journeys, information architecture, and user flows
- Design visual interfaces and design systems
- Conduct or contribute to user research and usability testing
- Provide design specifications and documentation to Developers
- Ensure accessibility standards (WCAG) are met in designs

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, user-centered product experiences
- Reduce friction in user workflows
- Maintain visual consistency and brand alignment

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews with Product Managers and stakeholders
- Design handoff documentation and design system specs
- Collaboration with Developers on feasibility and implementation details

### Interactions with Other Roles
Partners with Product Managers on requirements and acceptance criteria, supports Developers during implementation with design guidance, and advocates for user needs throughout the project lifecycle.

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers plan, coordinate, and execute product releases across teams. They ensure all quality gates, dependencies, and communication requirements are met before deployment.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain release plans, timelines, and dependencies
- Coordinate with QA, Developers, and infrastructure teams on release readiness
- Verify completion of release checklists and quality gates
- Manage release communications, stakeholder briefings, and user announcements
- Coordinate rollback plans and contingency procedures

### Goals
- Execute releases on schedule with zero unplanned issues
- Maintain quality and reliability of deployments
- Provide transparency and confidence to stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews and go/no-go decisions
- Deployment playbooks and runbooks
- Pre-release and post-release status updates

### Interactions with Other Roles
Coordinates with Project Managers on timeline and resources, works with QA to verify testing completion, partners with Developers for deployment execution, and keeps Stakeholders informed of release status and any blockers.

---

## Support Lead

### Role Summary
Support Leads manage post-release support activities, incident escalation, and rapid response to production issues. They serve as the bridge between end-users, engineering, and stakeholders during incidents.

### Responsibilities
- Monitor production systems and user-reported issues post-deployment
- Triage, prioritize, and escalate production incidents
- Coordinate rapid responses and hotfix development with Developers
- Manage incident communication to users and stakeholders
- Facilitate post-incident reviews and retrospectives for production events

### Goals
- Minimize time-to-resolution (MTTR) for production issues
- Maintain user trust through transparent incident communication
- Extract learnings from incidents to prevent recurrence

### Typical Communication
- Incident triage and severity assessments
- Real-time incident status updates to stakeholders
- Post-incident blameless retrospective facilitation

### Interactions with Other Roles
Works with Developers and QA to triage and resolve production bugs, coordinates with Project Managers on impact assessment, communicates with Stakeholders on incident status and timelines, and leverages Project Manager and Product Manager insights for long-term fixes vs. hotfixes.
